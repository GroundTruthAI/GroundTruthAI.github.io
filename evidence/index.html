Evidence

Evidence, stated plainly

Business-led positioning must not become exaggerated claims. Every statement on this site is classified by the kind of evidence behind it, and every business case states what has not yet been proven.

How we classify evidence

  • Demonstrated business outcome

    Measured in a customer production environment.

    Today: None yet

  • Experimentally supported technical outcome

    Measured in internal experiments on public benchmark datasets.

    Today: Data reduction and accuracy on 4 benchmarks; internal energy measurements

  • Proposed enterprise application

    Architecture and value hypothesis, designed to be tested in a pilot.

    Today: All 11 business cases

  • Illustrative financial scenario

    Arithmetic on stated, editable assumptions. Not a forecast or a quote.

Energy measurements

  • 86% less energy per model training run on a workstation after data reduction.
  • 39% less energy on IoT / edge devices at the point of capture.
  • Internal measurements; energy estimated with Schneider Electric reference data. Methodology not yet published.
  • The figures do not separately report the energy consumed by the distillation step. Pilots measure it explicitly.

Read these results carefully

  • Small, clean, public datasets; classification only.
  • Not yet independently replicated.
  • Results do not transfer automatically to industrial time series, logs or multi-domain data.
  • No customer production deployment exists today.

Validation backlog

The experiments that would most strengthen the business cases. Planned; no dates are published.

  1. Industrial time-series benchmark — historian-style sensor data, failure prediction. Supports BC01, BC04, BC05.
  2. Multi-domain combination experiment — distill separately in heterogeneous domains, combine, compare with centralized training. Supports BC01, BC02, BC04, BC07, BC08, BC11.
  3. Rare-event coverage study — minority classes and failure modes after distillation. Supports BC01, BC04.
  4. Distillation overhead measurement — compute and energy of the distillation step itself. Supports every direct-cost claim.
  5. Retraining-cycle study — repeated distillation and retraining under drift. Supports BC09.
  6. Edge device characterization — footprint and throughput on representative gateways. Supports BC05.

Pilots are designed to generate exactly this evidence, on customer data, with measurements the customer keeps.
