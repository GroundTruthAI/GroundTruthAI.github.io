Industries

The same problem looks different in every sector

A business case describes a problem we can help solve. An industry describes where it occurs, with its own data estate, operating constraints and governance. Each industry page explains which cases matter most and why.

Industries

  • Manufacturing & industrial AI

    Plants generate high-frequency process and equipment data behind OT boundaries, while AI teams work centrally.

    5 relevant business cases

  • Energy, utilities & critical infrastructure

    Grid, generation and network operators combine strict OT security with geographically dispersed assets.

    4 relevant business cases

  • Oil, gas & mining

    Remote, harsh-environment operations with costly connectivity and strict separation of control systems.

    3 relevant business cases

  • Transportation & logistics

    Distributed fleets, terminals and networks produce telemetry that is costly to centralize in full.

    3 relevant business cases

  • Banking, insurance & financial services

    Large, regulated data estates split across entities, countries and platforms, with heavy model portfolios.

    7 relevant business cases

  • Healthcare & life sciences

    Sensitive data distributed across hospitals, labs and research partners, under strict protection rules.

    3 relevant business cases

  • Telecommunications

    Network and device data at massive scale, produced at the edge and processed centrally.

    5 relevant business cases

  • Retail & consumer

    High-volume transactional and behavioral data feeding many frequently retrained models.

    3 relevant business cases

  • Industrial groups & multinationals

    Decentralized subsidiaries, regions and acquisitions, each with its own data platform and rules.

    5 relevant business cases

  • AI infrastructure, cloud & data center providers

    Providers and platform teams whose margins depend on storage, compute and energy efficiency.

    4 relevant business cases
