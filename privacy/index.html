2. Data we process

We only process the data you provide through the contact form: name, work email, organization, role, industry, business case of interest, expected timeline and any description you choose to include. Please do not include confidential information or personal data about other people.

This website uses no cookies and no analytics tools. The hosting provider may record IP addresses in its technical logs for security purposes.

3. Purpose and legal basis

To respond to your request (initial conversation, feasibility assessment, pilot, technology partnership or technical review) and, where applicable, to manage the pre-contractual relationship. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time, and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not profile you or make automated decisions.

4. Recipients and processors

We do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. The following providers act as processors to deliver the service: the FormSubmit form service, which delivers your request by email; the email provider where it is received; and GitHub Pages, which hosts the website. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; any such transfer must rely on the safeguards set out in Chapter V of the GDPR.

5. Retention

We keep your data for as long as needed to respond to your request and, afterwards, blocked for the periods required by law to handle possible liabilities.

6. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability, and withdraw your consent, by writing to ingenieros.informatica@gmail.com. If you believe the processing does not comply with the law, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or the supervisory authority of your country.
