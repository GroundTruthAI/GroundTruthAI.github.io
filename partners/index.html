Partners and OEM

Embed source-side distillation in your product

GroundTruth AI is a software component designed to sit inside other architectures. For platforms that already run next to industrial, edge or enterprise data, it adds a differentiated capability: task-specific training datasets produced at the source.

Discuss a technology integration

Why it adds value to an existing product

  • Your customers want central AI on data your product already holds or moves, without replicating all of it.
  • Source-side distillation turns that data into approved, task-specific training datasets inside your product's footprint.
  • It complements, rather than replaces, automation, historians, data integration and security infrastructure.
  • It creates a new reason to keep data processing in your platform rather than exporting raw data elsewhere.

Where it can be embedded

  • Industrial automation platforms
  • Industrial historians
  • OT/IT integration platforms
  • Industrial gateways
  • Edge computing platforms
  • Enterprise data integration products
  • Cloud infrastructure platforms
  • MLOps platforms
  • AI development infrastructure

Where GroundTruth AI sits inside a partner product

  • Raw data
  • Distilled dataset
  • Model / results
  • Control / approvals
  • GroundTruth AI component

A proposed integration pattern, not an existing integration. Compatibility with a partner product is claimed only after it has been built and tested together.

Commercial models

  • Embedded software licensing

    The runtime ships inside your product and runs where your product already runs.

  • OEM licensing

    Source-side distillation offered under your brand as a feature or module of your platform.

  • Technology licensing

    Access to the method for a defined field of use, under NDA and license terms.

  • Joint technical validation

    A shared experiment plan on your target data before any co-marketing claim.

  • Joint enterprise solutions

    A combined offer for a business case such as OT/IT data segregation or edge-to-cloud.

  • Distribution partnerships

    Systems integrators and resellers delivering assessments, pilots and rollouts.

Licensing terms are discussed per partnership; no prices are published.
