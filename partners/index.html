Partnership models

GroundTruth AI has no announced partnerships today. The models below describe what we are seeking.

  • Systems integrators and consultancies

    Co-deliver pilots and production rollouts inside OT, data platform and MLOps programs.

    We bring

    Reference architectures, pilot methodology and technical enablement.

  • Industrial automation and OT vendors

    Embed source-side distillation in historians, edge gateways or industrial data platforms (OEM).

    We bring

    Embedded runtime licensing and joint validation on industrial data.

  • Cloud and data platforms

    Integrate distilled datasets into data, ML and marketplace offerings for multicloud and residency scenarios.

    We bring

    Integration design for object stores, pipelines and registries.

  • Infrastructure and hardware providers

    Pair efficient training-data pipelines with workstation, server and data-center offerings.

    We bring

    Joint measurement of training time and energy on partner hardware.

  • Research and validation partners

    Independent replication and new benchmarks: time series, multi-domain, rare events.

    We bring

    Access to the method under NDA for structured evaluation.

OEM and embedded licensing

Several business cases (OT/IT segregation, edge-to-cloud, distributed industrial intelligence) are strongest when distillation ships inside a platform the customer already trusts. We offer the runtime for embedding, with joint validation on the partner's target data.

  • Licensing models are discussed per partnership; no prices are published.
  • Joint validation plan before any co-marketing claim.
  • No claim of compatibility with a partner product until it has been tested.
