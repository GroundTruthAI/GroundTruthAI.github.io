Partners and OEM

Embed source-side distillation in your product

For platforms that already run next to industrial, edge or enterprise data, GroundTruth AI can add a differentiated capability: task-specific training datasets produced where the data lives.

Discuss a technology integration

Where it could be embedded

  • Industrial data platforms and historians

    Distill where plant data is already collected, before it is exported.

  • Industrial edge environments

    Reduce what leaves the site for central training.

  • Data integration architectures

    Move task-specific datasets instead of full replicas between domains.

  • Enterprise AI infrastructure and MLOps

    Train and retrain on validated distilled datasets.

  • Distributed analytical systems

    Enable central models across domains that keep their data.

GroundTruth AI complements automation, historians, data integration and security infrastructure; it does not replace them.

Proposed integration pattern

  • Original (raw) data
  • Optimized (distilled) dataset
  • Model / results
  • Approved process (not a data path)
  • GroundTruth AI component

Today GroundTruth AI is an experimental Python runtime available for evaluation. No integration with a third-party product has been built, and none is claimed until it has been built and tested together. No partnerships have been announced.

How we work with technology partners

  • Joint technical validation on your target data, before any joint claim.
  • OEM, embedded or technology licensing for a defined field of use, discussed per partnership. No prices are published.
  • Joint solutions for a business case such as OT/IT data segregation or edge-to-cloud optimization.
  • Delivery partnerships with systems integrators for assessments and pilots.
