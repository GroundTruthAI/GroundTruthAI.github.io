    Outcome

  • Distributed enterprise intelligence · Flagship

    Cross-enterprise analytics

    Problem

    Group-wide analytics stall because subsidiaries, regions and business units hold data in separate, separately governed estates.

    Outcome

    Enterprise-wide predictive models without a complete data consolidation program.

    Banking, insurance & financial services · Healthcare & life sciences · Industrial groups & multinationals

  • AI infrastructure efficiency · Flagship

    AI training cost optimization

    Problem

    Training and retraining on ever-larger datasets drives compute spend, slows experimentation and ties up shared infrastructure.

    Outcome

    Lower compute per training run and faster experimentation, within an agreed accuracy tolerance.

    Banking, insurance & financial services · Telecommunications · Retail & consumer · …

  • Distributed enterprise intelligence · Flagship

    Distributed industrial intelligence

    Problem

    Failure patterns are scattered across plants with different equipment and conditions, and centralizing all their history is impractical.

    Outcome

    Shared predictive models that learn across facilities and operating conditions.

    Manufacturing & industrial AI · Energy, utilities & critical infrastructure · Oil, gas & mining · …

  • Distributed enterprise intelligence

    Data residency and sovereignty

    Problem

    Multinational and regulated organizations must keep original data in specific jurisdictions or domains, which limits shared analytics.

    Outcome

    Less cross-border replication of complete datasets, with source data ownership preserved.

    Banking, insurance & financial services · Healthcare & life sciences · Industrial groups & multinationals · …

  • Distributed enterprise intelligence

    Post-merger analytical integration

    Problem

    Merged organizations want joint analytics early, but platform integration and data migration take years.

    Outcome

    Earlier joint analytical capabilities with less dependency on complete data migration.

    Industrial groups & multinationals · Banking, insurance & financial services · Healthcare & life sciences

  • AI infrastructure efficiency

    Continuous model retraining

    Problem

    Production models are retrained on ever-growing full histories, so each cycle costs more compute, time and storage than the last.

    Outcome

    Lower recurring retraining cost and shorter model update cycles, with fidelity checked every cycle.

    Banking, insurance & financial services · Retail & consumer · Telecommunications · …

  • AI infrastructure efficiency

    AI infrastructure and data center efficiency

    Problem

    Enterprise AI workloads consume growing storage, compute and energy, and much of that capacity is spent on redundant training data.

    Outcome

    Better utilization of AI infrastructure for eligible workloads, net of distillation overhead.

    AI infrastructure, cloud & data center providers · Telecommunications · Banking, insurance & financial services

  • AI infrastructure efficiency

    Sustainable AI infrastructure

    Problem

    AI workloads add energy demand that sustainability teams must account for, but few levers reduce it without reducing AI ambitions.

    Outcome

    A measurable, workload-level reduction in training energy that can feed sustainability reporting once verified.

    AI infrastructure, cloud & data center providers · Industrial groups & multinationals · Energy, utilities & critical infrastructure

  • Intelligent data mobility

    Edge-to-cloud data optimization

    Problem

    Sensors, cameras and edge systems generate more data than networks, cloud ingestion and central storage can economically absorb.

    Outcome

    Less network traffic and cloud ingestion for AI training, with a scalable distributed architecture.

    Telecommunications · Manufacturing & industrial AI · Energy, utilities & critical infrastructure · …

  • Intelligent data mobility

    Multicloud data optimization

    Problem

    AI workloads span private infrastructure and several clouds, forcing costly duplication and transfer of large training datasets.

    Outcome

    Less data duplicated and moved between environments for AI workloads.

    Banking, insurance & financial services · Retail & consumer · AI infrastructure, cloud & data center providers · …
