Why we exist

Enterprise data is distributed for good reasons: safety, ownership, regulation, cost. The default AI workflow ignores those reasons and asks for everything in one place. We think the better answer is to move less, and to move only what a model actually needs.

Where we are

  • Proof-of-concept-ready technology, validated in internal experiments on public benchmarks.
  • Seeking structured enterprise pilots and design partners.
  • No production deployments, customer references or certifications yet. We say so on every business case.

Founded by

Applied machine-learning researchers with backgrounds in data-efficient learning, decision systems and industrial and enterprise AI.
