Company

Keep data distributed. Centralize intelligence.

GroundTruth AI enables enterprise intelligence across distributed and segregated data environments while reducing unnecessary data movement and optimizing AI infrastructure efficiency.

Discuss your use case

Why we exist

Enterprise data is distributed for good reasons: safety, ownership, regulation, cost. The default AI workflow ignores those reasons and asks for everything in one place. We think the better answer is to move less, and to move only what a model actually needs.

  • Distributed enterprise intelligence — Enable enterprise-wide intelligence without centralizing all your raw data.
  • AI infrastructure efficiency — Reduce the cost of developing, training and maintaining AI models.
  • Intelligent data mobility — Move the information your AI needs, not every raw data record.

Where we are

  • Technology validated in internal experiments on public benchmarks.
  • A prototype runtime available for evaluation in customer environments.
  • An enterprise pilot program open to organizations with a concrete distributed-data problem.
  • Production deployments, customer references and certifications will be published here once they exist.

Expertise

Founded by applied machine-learning researchers with backgrounds in data-efficient learning, decision systems and industrial and enterprise AI.

Technical due diligence, including method details under NDA, is available on request.
