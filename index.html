The opportunity

The centralize-everything pattern is breaking

Segregation is deliberate

OT networks, subsidiaries, regions and clouds are separated for safety, security, ownership and regulation. AI programs that require a full raw copy in one place collide with those decisions.

Consolidation is slow and expensive

Migrating and harmonizing entire data estates takes years. Valuable analytics wait for programs whose scope is far larger than any single model needs.

Training cost scales with data

Every retraining cycle reprocesses history. Storage, transfer, compute and energy grow with volume, while much of that volume adds little to the model.

The opportunity: prepare the data a model actually needs where the data lives, and move only that, under the controls you already have.

Proposed solution

How it works

  1. 1

    Define the task

    Target model, variables and acceptance thresholds agreed with data owners.

  2. 2

    Distill at the source

    A runtime inside the authorized environment builds a compact, task-specific training set.

  3. 3

    Validate fidelity

    Models trained on full and distilled data are compared on the same test set.

  4. 4

    Approve and transfer

    Owners approve; the dataset moves through existing, governed paths.

  5. 5

    Train centrally

    Central teams train and iterate on a fraction of the volume.
