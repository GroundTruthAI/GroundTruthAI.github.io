GroundTruth AI

Executive overview

Keep data distributed. Centralize intelligence.

GroundTruth AI is software that works inside each plant, business unit or region. It builds a much smaller version of the data that contains what an AI model needs for a specific purpose. Only that smaller dataset is shared; the original data stays where it is.

  • 1Enable new AI and analytics projects
  • 2Reduce unnecessary data movement
  • 3Optimize AI infrastructure costs

Dataset distillation, not compression. Compression shrinks the same data, which must be unpacked before use, so the AI still processes all of it. Distillation builds a much smaller dataset that the AI uses directly to learn.

Proof point. In internal tests on four public datasets, models trained on 5–14% of the data came within 1.4–2.8 accuracy points of models trained on all of it.

GroundTruth AI

Your data is spread out. Your AI needs all of it.

Large companies want AI that learns from every plant, subsidiary and country. But their data lives in separate systems, and copying all of it to one place is often slow, expensive or not allowed.

  • Security boundaries

    Industrial control systems are kept apart from corporate IT, so plant data cannot simply be copied out.

  • Data protection and residency

    Customer and personal data often has to stay in its country or company.

  • Cost and volume

    Moving and storing terabytes again and again costs money, network capacity and time.

  • Constant retraining

    AI models need regular updates, and each update reprocesses an ever-larger history.

The result: valuable AI projects are delayed, scaled down or never started.

  2. Reduce the cost of AI

    • Lower computing costs
    • Cheaper, faster model updates

    • From industrial systems (OT) to corporate IT
    • From sensors and remote sites to the cloud
    • Between different cloud providers

GroundTruth AI

20 industrial plants. One AI model.

Imagine a global manufacturer that wants to anticipate equipment failures across all its facilities while keeping original operational data within its existing industrial environments.

Enterprise-wide predictive maintenance without complete raw data consolidation.

How it works, step by step

  1. Each plant keeps its historical data where it is
  2. GroundTruth AI, installed in each plant, prepares a much smaller dataset for the maintenance model
  3. After review and approval, only that dataset travels through the existing connection
  4. The central team trains one model with data from all 20 plants
