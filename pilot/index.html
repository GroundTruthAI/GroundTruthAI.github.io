Start with a short conversation about your architecture. Move to a feasibility assessment and an enterprise pilot only when the case is clear.

Three steps, each with a clear decision

  1. Stage 1

    Architecture discovery

    A short conversation about your business problem, data architecture, segregation requirements, current AI workloads and constraints, and whether GroundTruth AI is a plausible fit. No data is shared.

    Contact

  2. Stage 2

    Technical feasibility assessment

    A scoped technical evaluation of one representative workload: data characteristics, existing baseline, integration requirements, the dataset reduction worth testing, quality constraints and preliminary economics.

    Request a feasibility assessment

  3. Stage 3

    Enterprise pilot

    A structured evaluation in your environment with agreed business objectives and technical success criteria, ending with an architecture assessment, an economic evaluation and deployment recommendations.

    Request an enterprise pilot

Pilots run in your environment, against your baseline, with success criteria you set before they start. Pricing is scoped per engagement and not published.
