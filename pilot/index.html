Request a Pilot

A bounded evaluation on one workload, in your environment, against your baseline, with success criteria you set before we start.

How a pilot runs

  1. 1

    Scope

    Pick one workload and baseline. Agree technical and economic acceptance thresholds.

  2. 2

    Deploy

    Install the runtime in the authorized environment. Review placement with security.

  3. 3

    Distill and validate

    Compare full-data and distilled-data models on the same test set.

  4. 4

    Measure and decide

    Transfer, storage, compute, energy and accuracy, including distillation overhead.

Indicative duration 8–12 weeks. Pilot pricing is scoped per engagement and not published.

Tell us about your situation

We reply with a scoping call proposal and the pilot design for your business case. If distillation is not a good fit, we will say so.

  • Which data, where it lives, and why it cannot simply be centralized
  • The model or analytics you want to build
  • The baseline you would compare against
